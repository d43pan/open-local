# Handling sensitive local information

In a small place, a name in a public record is a neighbour. Being public does not make
something right to repeat or to spread further. These rules apply even when the source is
official.

## Do not include

- Home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, licence plates or other contact details
  of private individuals.
- Names of minors, of victims of crime, or of people in medical, mental-health or welfare
  situations.
- Police logs and arrest reports naming private individuals. An arrest is an allegation.
  If a public-safety matter is relevant, describe the event ("police responded to a crash
  on Main Road") without naming private people, and link the official source.
- Names in routine records where the person is incidental: who pulled a building permit
  for their house, who owes taxes, who was in probate court, who filed a complaint about a
  neighbour. Describe the matter by its public aspect (the project, the parcel, the board's
  action) instead.
- Rumour, speculation, and unverified claims from forums or social media.

## Fine to include

- Public officials, candidates, and employees acting in their official roles.
- Businesses, organisations and their publicly named representatives.
- People named in their own announcements (an artist's show, a farmer's stand hours).
- Applicants before a public board, when the application itself is the news (a
  subdivision, a liquor licence), named as the record names them.

## Take extra care

- Obituaries and death notices: only if asked, briefly, linking the family's or funeral
  home's notice.
- Emergencies in progress: point to the official source and its time. Do not relay
  unconfirmed details.
- Anything that could direct attention or harassment at a private person or a small group.

## The registry itself

The registry holds sources, not people. It carries no personal contact details, and
contributors are identified by a public handle of their choosing or not at all. Private
groups, closed forums and personal social media accounts are not listed.

When unsure, leave the name out. The fact usually stands without it.
