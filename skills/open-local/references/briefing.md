# The shape of an answer

Match the size of the answer to the question. "When does the library open on Saturday?"
gets one cited line and a one-line coverage note. "What happened this week?" gets the
full shape below.

```
# <Place>: <what was asked>, <date range>
As of <today's date>. Sources: <n> read, <n> could not be read.

## What happened
- **<Plain statement of the fact>.** <One sentence of context in your own words.>
  <Kind: decision | record | reporting | allegation>. Published <date>.
  Source: [<Source name>](<link>) (first-hand | reporting by <newsroom>)[, paywalled]
  [Also covered by: <name>]

## Coming up
- **<Day, date, time>: <event or meeting>**, <where>. <One line: what is on the agenda,
  marked as proposed.> Source: [<name>](<link>)

## Food, arts and around town        (only when asked, or for a general "what's on")
- **<Fact>** (checked <date>). Source: [<name>](<link>)

## Worth knowing                      (optional: deadlines, hearings, closures)

## Coverage
- Read: <the sources actually read, by name>
- Could not read: <name: why> …
- Not checked by this briefing: <kinds of source the registry lacks for this place>
- Not in the registry: <anything found by web search, labelled>
- This is what these sources published. It is not everything that happened.
```

## Notes

- Lead with the place's own sources (`relation: direct`), then area-wide items that
  actually mention the place. Do not pad with statewide news that has no local tie; if
  you include a regional item, say why it is relevant.
- One line per fact. Group the same story from several outlets into one line.
- Dates in full ("Thursday 3 December 2026"), in the place's own time zone.
- Keep quotations to a phrase or a sentence, in quotation marks, with the link.
- If the window is quiet, say so and still give "Coming up" and "Coverage".
- For "within N miles" answers, name the town for every item.
- End by offering one concrete next step: a narrower question, a longer window, or
  adding a missing source to the registry.
